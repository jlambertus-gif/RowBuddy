# Jurisdiction Requirements

Before launching in a city or country, RowBuddy must document:

- Whether queue-position transfers are lawful
- Whether the venue or organizer permits transfers
- Marketplace licensing requirements
- Payment and money-transmission implications
- Tax requirements
- Consumer-protection rules
- Refund obligations
- Privacy and evidence-retention requirements
- Age restrictions
- Dispute-resolution requirements

This review must also confirm that the line-standing service legal framing
(`docs/decisions/003-line-standing-service-framing.md`) holds locally, since
line-sitting precedent and anti-scalping/anti-touting statutes vary by
jurisdiction.

Jurisdiction gating is a data-driven concept, not a hardcoded rule: a
queue's country/category determines whether the platform can legally
operate there, and that determination can change over time as local law or
organizer policy changes.
