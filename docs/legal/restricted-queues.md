# Restricted Queues

The MVP must prohibit:

- Medical and emergency services
- Elections and voting
- Immigration and consular services
- Courts and legal proceedings
- Government benefits
- Essential public services
- School admissions
- Disaster relief
- Food aid
- Any queue where transfer is prohibited by law or organizer policy
- Any queue involving minors where safety cannot be assured

Jurisdiction-specific restrictions must be configurable, and are evaluated
both when a queue is submitted for approval and when an auction is created
against an approved queue (see
`docs/decisions/005-hybrid-queue-authorship.md`).
