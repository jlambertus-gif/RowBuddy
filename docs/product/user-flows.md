# User Flows

## Seller

1. Register and verify account.
2. Find or create a queue (creation may require admin approval — see
   `docs/decisions/005-hybrid-queue-authorship.md`).
3. Confirm physical presence.
4. Upload evidence.
5. Enter approximate position.
6. Create auction.
7. Remain near the queue.
8. Receive bids.
9. Wait for winner payment.
10. Transfer position using a temporary QR code.
11. Confirm transfer.
12. Receive payout after the protection period.

## Buyer

1. Register and verify account.
2. Find nearby queues.
3. Review auction and confidence signals.
4. Place bid.
5. Win and pay (payment authorized at win, captured at transfer
   confirmation).
6. Travel to transfer location.
7. Present temporary QR code.
8. Confirm receipt.
9. Rate seller or open dispute.
