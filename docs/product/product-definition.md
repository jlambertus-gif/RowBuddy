# Product Definition

## Product statement

RowBuddy is a multilingual marketplace that allows a verified person who
is physically present in an eligible queue to auction their position to
another user who wants to save waiting time.

## Value proposition

- Sellers monetize time already spent waiting.
- Buyers save time by bidding for a verified position.
- RowBuddy provides verification, bidding, payment protection, transfer,
  auditability, and dispute handling.

## Product limitation

RowBuddy does not own or grant queue positions. Acceptance of a transfer
may depend on the rules of the organizer, business, venue, or jurisdiction.
See `docs/decisions/003-line-standing-service-framing.md` for how this is
reflected in product and legal language.

## Initial users

- Seller
- Buyer
- Administrator
- Trust and safety operator
- Dispute operator

## Initial languages

- English
- Spanish
